1. Who may use FamilySafe

You must be 18 or older and a parent or legal guardian of the child when you create an account and when you install FamilySafe on the child’s phone. FamilySafe is not for children to self-register and is not part of Google Play’s Families / Designed for Families program.

2. The service

The app helps you limit apps, view location when needed, set study hours and watch zones, and receive alerts. It is a support tool. It does not replace care, pickup, or talking with your child.

3. Your account

You are responsible for your email, password, and signed-in devices. You may sign in with email/password or Google. You may delete your account at any time in the app or on the delete-account page.

4. Child devices

Pair only devices of a child you legally supervise. Do not use FamilySafe to track an adult, a partner, an employee, or anyone who is not under your guardianship or has not given lawful consent.

5. Android permissions

Some features need Usage access, Accessibility, and Location. Accessibility is only used to lock apps under parent rules — it is not an assistive disability tool. Location is requested as “While using the app”, not “Allow all the time”. You may turn permissions off in Android Settings; the related features will stop.

6. Accuracy

Location, battery, and app-time figures are the latest records, not a 100% live feed. No network, revoked permissions, indoor use, or low battery can delay or shift the numbers.

7. Responsibility

You are responsible for the rules you set on the child device and for telling the child in an age-appropriate way. FamilySafe is provided as-is. We do not promise the app will catch every event or prevent every risk.
